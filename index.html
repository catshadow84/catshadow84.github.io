About Me

I am a Computer Engineering student at the Lebanese American University (LAU) who turns real-world problems into AI-driven solutions. My work sits at the intersection of environmental data science and software engineering.

Whether I am building AquaVision, an AI pipeline that analyzes Sentinel-2 satellite imagery to monitor water quality across Lebanese water bodies, or Deadlock Diner, a visualizer that makes operating system algorithms accessible, my goal is the same: taking something complicated and making it usable.

My research on AquaVision has progressed to a manuscript currently under review at an international undergraduate research journal—a level of technical validation I am proud to have reached as an undergraduate. I plan to pursue a PhD, continuing to research how AI can address environmental and engineering challenges.

Alongside my technical work, I tutor peers in subjects ranging from Calculus to Discrete Structures, breaking down complex material into approachable, practical guides.

Technical Projects

AquaVision — AI Water-Quality Monitoring Pipeline

Python • Remote Sensing • Machine Learning • Research

The Problem: Monitoring cyanobacteria blooms in freshwater bodies is traditionally slow and expensive.

The Solution: An end-to-end Python pipeline (Pandas, GeoPandas, Rasterio, NumPy) that processes Sentinel-2 imagery and NASA CyFi data to detect blooms across five Lebanese freshwater sites.

The Impact: Engineered a composite water-health scoring and biodiversity risk-assessment module. Co-authored a manuscript currently under review at an International Journal of Undergraduate Research.

Airwatch Lebanon — AI Emissions Monitoring Dashboard

Flask • Google Earth Engine • Hackathon

The Context: Built during the Kanz AI Hackathon (LAU/MHRSD) under a strict deadline.

The Solution: A Flask dashboard using Sentinel-5P (TROPOMI) SO2/NO2 data via Google Earth Engine to monitor emissions from the Zouk Mikael and Jiyeh power plants.

The Impact: Repositioned remote-sensing tech for an industry-facing audience. Contributed to a Guinness World Record for the Largest Online AI Hackathon.

Deadlock Diner — Banker's Algorithm Visualizer

Operating Systems • Flask • Full-Stack

The Problem: Operating Systems concepts like the Banker's Algorithm are notoriously difficult for students to visualize.

The Solution: A full-stack Flask app with a Python safety-check engine, REST API, and an animated HTML/CSS/JavaScript front end.

The Impact: Demonstrates core CS fundamentals made accessible. A clean, focused tool for interactive OS education.
